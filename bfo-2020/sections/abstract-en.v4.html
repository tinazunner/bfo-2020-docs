Basic Formal Ontology (BFO) 2020 is a domain-neutral upper ontology designed to support the integration of scientific data and domain ontologies. BFO provides high-level categories—such as continuant vs. occurrent, material entity, process, role, function, disposition—and a small, realist axiomatization intended for reuse across disciplines. This site presents a developer-friendly, navigable specification generated directly from the official OWL release (2019-08-26), including cross-references and downloadable serializations.

  • Scope: domain-neutral categories and relations for scientific knowledge bases
  • Design: realist commitments; minimal axiomatization; interoperability-first
  • Use: adopted widely across the OBO Foundry and related ontology ecosystems
