BFO serves as a top-level framework that domain ontologies import to maintain consistent categories and relations. It distinguishes continuants (enduring entities such as material entities, qualities, and roles) from occurrents (processes and temporal regions), and provides relations that connect them. BFO intentionally contains only domain-neutral content; it is not itself a catalog of biomedical, geological, or social entities, but a scaffold those ontologies reuse.

This documentation is intended for developers and ontology engineers. It provides: (i) human-readable descriptions; (ii) cross-references for classes, object properties, and annotations; and (iii) links to download BFO in multiple RDF serializations (RDF/XML, Turtle, JSON-LD, N-Triples). For background and methodology, see the BFO guidebook and OBO Foundry resources.
